Priorities for future research Future research should focus on further analysing neonatal deaths in private facilities, conducting comparative investigations in Luuka district and exploring the effectiveness of interventions aimed at reducing delays and improving neonatal care. Research on the impact of cultural and social dynamics on care-seeking behaviours, as well as the effectiveness of community engagement through CHWs, could provide valuable insights. Continued examination of healthcare infrastructure, quality control in private facilities, and the impact of maternal and community education programmes on care-seeking behaviours should be prioritised for a comprehensive understanding and sustainable interventions.
